Matches

    Points. A win is worth 3 league points, a draw 1, a loss 0. The match score is the placement points from both rounds: 5 for 1st, 3 for 2nd, 2 for 3rd, 0 for 4th.

    Tiebreakers. Points difference first, then points scored.

    Promotion and relegation. The top 2 of the Gold League win a prize. The bottom 2 of the Gold League go down to the Silver League, and the top 2 of the Silver League go up.

    Live matches count with their current score. Untick “Count live matches” to see the table without them.
